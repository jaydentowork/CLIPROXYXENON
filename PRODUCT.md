# XENEON EDGE

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
A personal, at-a-glance monitoring display on the CORSAIR XENEON EDGE at 2560 × 720. The user checks remaining provider quota, selected-period token use, and recent completed requests through a browser. Requirements are approved in docs/dashboard-brief.md.

## Product character
Calm, direct, legible. A dark monitoring surface for a desk touchscreen, with enough contrast to read without sustained attention.

## Design principles
- Keep the three provider summaries and live feed visible together.
- Report partial coverage, stale readings, and collection gaps honestly.
- Keep calendar token reporting distinct from provider quota resets.
- Make Today, Week, and Month controls easy to touch and operate by keyboard.
- Use native browser elements and reserve visual emphasis for useful state.

## Scope and accessibility
Display only; no account-management controls or app-specific login. At least 44 × 44 CSS-pixel interactive targets, visible focus, reduced motion, readable text contrast, and text labels alongside status colors. The narrower layout remains usable; EDGE is the primary target.
